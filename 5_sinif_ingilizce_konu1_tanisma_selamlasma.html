5th Grade English • Topic 1

GREETING & MEETING PEOPLE

1. Konu: Tanışma, Selamlaşma, Kendini ve Başkalarını Tanıtma
🎯 Conversation HeroIntroduce yourself, make international friends and ask about hobbies & subjects!

💬 4 Steps of Meeting Someone New

1. Greet (Selamla)
"Hello! / Hi there!"
2. Introduce (Tanıt)
"My name is..." / "I am..."
3. Be Glad (Memnun Ol)
"Nice to meet you!"
4. Share (Paylaş)
Age, Country, School

🔑 6 Temel Kavram (Kavramlar Kutusu)

1. Introduce Yourself

"Hello, my name is Efe. I am 10 years old and I am from Türkiye." (Kendini tanıtma).

2. Introduce Others

"This is my friend, Maria. She is from Spain. She is 11." (Arkadaşını tanıtma: 'This is...').

3. Country (Ülke)

Devlet adıdır. Daima büyük harfle yazılır: Türkiye, England, Germany, France, Japan, Italy.

4. Nationality (Uyruk)

Kişinin vatandaşı olduğu ırk/millet: Turkish, English, German, French, Japanese, Italian.

5. School Subjects

Okul dersleri: Maths, Science, Social Studies, English, Music, Art, PE (Beden Eğitimi), Drama.

6. Expressing Likes & Dislikes

"I like English and Art, but I dislike Maths." / "My favourite subject is Music."

📝 Sample Dialogue (Örnek Diyalog)

  • 👦 Tom: Hello! My name is Tom. What is your name?
  • 👧 Zeynep: Hi Tom! I'm Zeynep. Nice to meet you.
  • 👦 Tom: Nice to meet you, too! Where are you from, Zeynep?
  • 👧 Zeynep: I am from Türkiye. I am Turkish. How about you?
  • 👦 Tom: I am from the UK. I speak English and a little Spanish.
  • 👧 Zeynep: Great! What is your favourite class at school?
  • 👦 Tom: I love Science and PE, but I don't like History.

🔍 Essential Question Patterns

  • ❓ Ad: "What is your name?" → "My name is..."
  • ❓ Yaş: "How old are you?" → "I am 10 years old."
  • ❓ Memleket: "Where are you from?" → "I am from..."
  • ❓ Milliyet: "What nationality are you?" → "I am..."
  • ❓ Dil: "Which languages do you speak?" → "I speak..."
  • ❓ Ders Sevgisi: "Do you like Maths?" → "Yes, I do. / No, I don't."

⚠️ Common Exam Traps & Pitfalls (Sınav Tuzakları)

1. "This is" Kullanımı: Yanımızdaki birini başkasına tanıtırken "He is my friend" yerine ilk tanışmada "This is my friend Mark" kalıbı kullanılır.
2. "Speak" ile "Language": "How many languages do you speak?" sorusuna rakamla ve dil adıyla yanıt verilir. "I speak two languages: Turkish and English."
3. "Nice to meet you" Karşılığı: Karşı taraf "Nice to meet you" dediğinde cevap "Nice to meet you, too" (Ben de memnun oldum) olur.
